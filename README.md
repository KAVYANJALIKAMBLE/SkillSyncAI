## 🌟 About SkillSyncAI

**SkillSyncAI** is an AI-powered career development platform designed to help students and developers understand their technical skills, identify skill gaps, receive personalized career guidance, and track their professional growth.

The platform combines **GitHub activity analysis, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, NLP, career recommendations, and interactive analytics** into one unified system.

> 💡 **SkillSyncAI transforms your coding activity into actionable career insights.**

---

## 🎯 Why SkillSyncAI?

Traditional career platforms often provide generic recommendations.

SkillSyncAI aims to provide **personalized insights based on your actual technical profile and development activity.**
